# Lumen Rack Collective — Season 2026

Public showcase for the collective's current instrument line.

- [Wavefolder](wavefolder.md) — Voltage-controlled wavefolder with stepped symmetry and an AC-coupled input stage.
- [Heliotrope VCO](heliotrope-vco.md) — Through-zero voltage-controlled oscillator with a temperature-compensated exponential converter.
- [Orrery Sequencer](orrery-sequencer.md) — Eight-lane Euclidean step sequencer with per-lane clock division and a transpose bus.
- [Driftglass Fold](driftglass-fold.md) — Six-stage diode wave folder with a dry/wet crossfade and clickless bypass.
- [Perigee Mixer](perigee-mixer.md) — Four-channel summing mixer with voltage-controlled pan and a headphone cue bus.
